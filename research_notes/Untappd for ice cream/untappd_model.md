## Core features and mechanics (check-ins, badges, social, menus, database)

### Takeaway
Untappd is a "Foursquare for beer": the atomic unit is a check-in (one drink, optionally tied to a venue, a rating, a photo and tasting notes). Gamification (badges with levels, some paid for by brands) drives repeat logging. A crowdsourced database, where users add missing items, regional moderators merge duplicates and brewery owners claim and curate their pages, keeps the catalog complete. Paying venues get "Verified" status and their live menus shown in the app.

### Cited Findings
- **Concept origin:** the founders were active Foursquare users and modelled Untappd on its check-in behaviour, "with more emphasis on what check-ins could do for beer drinkers" — [Braze/Appboy case study](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/engage-millions-users-mobile-community-building-lessons-untappd); [CNBC 2016](https://www.cnbc.com/2016/04/25/two-guys-created-their-dream-jobs-forged-by-a-love-for-tech-and-beer.html)
- **Badges, general:** some badges let users "level up" — [Untappd Lounge, All About Untappd Badges (2023)](https://lounge.untappd.com/everything-you-need-to-know-about-untappd-badges). Public profiles show standard badges at high levels, such as "Brewery Pioneer" at Level 15 and higher, and "Beer of the World (Level 6)" — [example profile](https://untappd.com/user/Schmidty3000/badges); [example profile](https://untappd.com/user/rapurcell85/badges)
- **Badges, style-based:** badges are awarded for checking in certain styles of beer — [VinePair](https://vinepair.com/articles/untapped-impact-craft-beer/). Untappd issues new badge sets each year, such as "New Badges for 2021" and seasonal badges like "Winter Beer Gear 2025" — [Untappd blog 2021](https://untappd.com/blog/new-badges-for-2021/1213); [Untappd blog 2025](https://untappd.com/blog/new-badge-winter-beer-gear-2025/1863)
- **Sponsored badges:** these are "normally sponsored by a brewery or a business with a fixed timeline and specific criteria to unlock" — [Untappd Lounge 2023](https://lounge.untappd.com/everything-you-need-to-know-about-untappd-badges). Eligible sponsors are regionally or nationally distributed breweries, beer-week organisers and ad agencies. Single short events such as festivals do not qualify. Requests go to business@untappd.com at least a month ahead — [Untappd Help Center](https://help.untappd.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034036152)
- **Sponsored badge results (vendor claims):** Untappd says sponsoring businesses typically see a 50–100% increase in check-ins. Lagunitas ran a three-badge series with 40,223 unlocks in one campaign — [Untappd Marketing, Lagunitas](https://marketing.untappd.com/success-story/lagunitas-brewing-company/); [UTFB case study](https://utfb.untappd.com/case-study/lagunitas-increase-beer-sales-build-brand-loyalty-case-study/). Flying Saucer's 2024 Belgian Beer Week badge was unlocked 804 times and tied to $29,571 in sales across 11 locations — [Flying Saucer case study PDF (2025)](https://marketing.untappd.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/untappd-marketing-flying-saucer-case-study-001.pdf)
- **Activity feed:** Untappd 2.2 (Feb 2013) shipped a new activity feed and better beer discovery on Android and iOS. Push notifications followed in July 2013 — [MobileSyrup 2013](https://mobilesyrup.com/2013/02/06/untappd-2-2-debuts-for-android-and-ios-with-new-activity-feed-and-improved-beer-discovery/); [MobileSyrup Jul 2013](https://mobilesyrup.com/2013/07/11/untappd-brings-push-notifications-to-its-iphone-and-android-apps/)
- **Database, adding items:** users can add beers they check in that are not yet in the system, and breweries can add their own — [Help Center, Breweries: manage beers](https://help.untappd.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033797752-Breweries-How-do-I-manage-my-beers-on-Untappd)
- **Database, duplicates:** any user can "Propose Duplicate" on a drink page, and the proposal goes to a Moderator for that region. Claimed breweries merge their own duplicates in a Beer Management tab: they pick a master listing and give a reason, and an Admin approves the merge. Cross-brewery duplicates, such as collaborations or guest taps, go through support — [Help Center](https://help.untappd.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033797752-Breweries-How-do-I-manage-my-beers-on-Untappd); [Help Center, unique count decreased after merges](https://help.untappd.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034134252-My-unique-beer-count-has-decreased-all-of-a-sudden-What-happened)
- **Moderator tiers (community reports, anecdotal):** r/Untappd users describe tiered moderators (M2, M3). Only M3 can see the full merge list, and even M3 cannot reverse merges. The subreddit runs a weekly "edit request" thread — [r/Untappd weekly edit request, Apr 2025](https://lr.psf.lt/r/Untappd/comments/1jyv79h/weekly_edit_request_post_20250414)
- **Brewery claiming:** claiming a brewery page is free. Owners can upload logos, add social links, edit and merge beers, see every check-in of their beers, and appoint "Brewery Managers" — [Help Center](https://help.untappd.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033797752-Breweries-How-do-I-manage-my-beers-on-Untappd)
- **Verified Venue:** venues are "Verified" only if they pay for Untappd for Business, and there is no free path — [BeerMenus guide (updated Oct 2025, competitor)](https://www.beermenus.com/blog/260-guide-to-untappd-for-business). Verified Venues can list menus, local badges and events, which unverified venues cannot — [Help Center](https://help.untappd.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033798072). Untappd also runs "Verified Venue Promotions" that surface paying venues to users — [Help Center](https://help.untappd.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037817491-Promotions-What-are-Verified-Venue-Promotions)
- **Expansion beyond beer:** in November 2025 the app opened to packaged and kegged beverages of all types. Users can now search, add, check in and rate them — [The Drinks Business, Nov 2025 (via search summary)](https://www.thedrinksbusiness.com/2025/11/untappd-expands-beyond-the-beer-category/); [American Craft Beer](https://www.americancraftbeer.com/?p=127921)
- **Annual recap ("Wrapped" analog):** "Untappd Recappd" launched on 15 Dec 2025. It offers personalised recaps of top styles, highest-rated drinks, flavor profiles and an interactive map, plus a faction-based challenge game. Content unlocks daily in the app and at recappd.untappd.com, and the campaign had a paying US sponsor (Crescent Canna) — [Recappd press release reprint, Dec 2025](https://rcnamericancsc.blogspot.com/2025/12/untappd-launches-untappd-recappd.html)
- **Community Awards:** "Untappd Community Awards" are annual rankings of top-rated beers built from user ratings: 2nd edition March 2025, 3rd edition 4 March 2026 — [BevNET PR, Mar 2025](https://www.bevnet.com/pr/2025/03/05/untappd-reveals-the-2nd-annual-untappd-community-awards-celebrating-the-worlds-toprated-beers); [CB Insights listing](https://www.cbinsights.com/investor/untappd)
- **Spotlights:** "Spotlights" showcases 3,000+ local favourites to promote small businesses — [Next Glass news](https://nextglass.co/news/untappd-launches-spotlights-to-showcase-3000-local-favorites-shining-a-light-on-small-business)

### Inferences
- Mechanics to copy for ice cream: (1) one-tap check-in of a specific flavour at a specific shop, (2) style and flavour-family badges with levels, which suit ice cream's enumerable bases, mix-ins and flavours, (3) free claiming of maker and shop pages, which turns makers into data stewards, (4) user-added items plus regional volunteer moderators for deduplication, and (5) a "Verified" paid tier that controls whether a live menu shows.
- Ice cream menus rotate faster than tap lists (daily or seasonal flavours), so live-menu sync, the Verified Venue analog, may matter more than in beer. Duplicate flavour names across shops ("Cookies & Cream" exists at every shop) mean items must be scoped to a maker, as Untappd scopes beers to breweries.

### Gaps
- Total number of badges and the maximum level cap: not found in sources reachable this session.
- Official documentation of toasts, wishlist ("Wish List"), flavor-tag vocabulary, serving-style options (draft/can/bottle) and the 0.25-step 5-point rating scale. These are core, widely known features, but no source surfaced in searches, so they are not cited here.
- No source found on the size or recruitment of the volunteer moderator corps.

## Growth history (founding, milestones, how it grew)

### Takeaway
Untappd went from a Twitter-born side project in 2010 to about 1 billion check-ins by 2021 and about 1.5 billion by late 2025. It reported nearly 12 million users and about 100 million check-ins in 2025. Growth rode the Foursquare check-in habit, the craft-beer boom and the ticker/badge community.

### Cited Findings
- **Founders:** Greg Avola (developer) met Tim Mather (designer) on Twitter when Avola was looking for a designer for a side project. The idea came in summer 2010, a private beta followed in September 2010, and public launch was 22 October 2010 (Wikipedia, via search summary) — [Braze](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/engage-millions-users-mobile-community-building-lessons-untappd); [Wine & More, 10 years of Untappd](https://www.wineandmore.com/stories/10-years-of-untappd-beer-drinking/)
- **Early roles:** CNBC (Apr 2016) described Mather as CEO (Los Angeles) and Avola as CTO. Both quit full-time jobs to work on Untappd only after the Next Glass merger — [CNBC 2016](https://www.cnbc.com/2016/04/25/two-guys-created-their-dream-jobs-forged-by-a-love-for-tech-and-beer.html)
- **2011:** Untappd hit 1 million check-ins and acquired competing beer app RedPint — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=311066)
- **Lean early ops:** "How One Developer Serves Millions of Beers," a case study of Untappd's infrastructure run largely by a single developer — [Iron.io blog](https://blog.iron.io/how-one-developer-serves-millions-of/)
- **2016:** more than 3 million users (podcast listing claim) — [Brad Garoon podcast, Sep 2016](https://bradgaroon.com/2016/09/15/utilizing-untappd)
- **2017:** 500,000,000 check-ins — [Untappd blog](https://blog.untappd.com/post/165869743736/cheers-to-500000000-check-ins)
- **2020:** 10th anniversary — [Untappd blog](https://untappd.com/blog/untappd-10th-anniversary-celebration/1149). An older Wikipedia figure of about 820,000 users dated to 2020 is clearly stale or inconsistent with the other data and should not be used (flagged in search summary of [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Untappd)).
- **2021:** 1 billionth check-in (April 2021 per Untappd Lounge), marked with a "Billionth Check-In Photo Contest" — [Untappd blog: about to hit one billion](https://untappd.com/blog/we-re-about-to-hit-one-billion-check-ins/1214); [photo contest](https://untappd.com/blog/introducing-untappd-s-billionth-check-in-photo-contest/1209); [Brewbound](https://www.brewbound.com/news/nextglass-ceo-shares-optimism-for-craft-recovery-during-ba-collab-hour)
- **2021 venue data:** Chicago led city venue check-ins (355,671), followed by Portland (326,607). Brewery-taproom share of check-ins rose from about 25% in mid-2019 to 27.1% in mid-2021 — [VinePair](https://vinepair.com/booze-news/top-10-cities-untappd-2021/); [Brewbound](https://www.brewbound.com/news/nextglass-ceo-shares-optimism-for-craft-recovery-during-ba-collab-hour)
- **Nov 2025:** more than 1.5 billion check-ins since 2010, at more than 1 million bars, restaurants, breweries and retailers — [The Drinks Business, Nov 2025 (via search summary)](https://www.thedrinksbusiness.com/2025/11/untappd-expands-beyond-the-beer-category/)
- **Dec 2025, latest public numbers:** nearly 12 million users received recaps. There were nearly 100 million check-ins in 2025, logging more than 1.5 million unique beverages across 2.6 million locations — [Recappd release reprint](https://rcnamericancsc.blogspot.com/2025/12/untappd-launches-untappd-recappd.html). The "~100M" figure comes from the headline only.
- **Ad reach (2025):** home-screen placements reach more than 43 million monthly impressions (Untappd marketing claim) — [Untappd B2C marketing](https://marketing.untappd.com/b2c-marketing-solutions/)

### Inferences
- Rough trajectory: 1M check-ins (2011), 500M (2017), 1B (2021), 1.5B (2025). That is about 100M per year at maturity, roughly flat, which suggests a mature, plateauing community rather than a hyper-growth one.
- The roughly 100M per year from about 12M users works out to about 8 check-ins per user per year on average, which implies a heavy-user core (tickers) plus a long tail of casual users. Ice cream is lower-frequency per person but broader in audience and family-friendly.
- The growth playbook is to borrow an existing behaviour (the check-in), attach it to an enthusiast hobby with collecting instincts (tickers), and use badges and recaps as shareable artifacts.

### Gaps
- Monthly active users and retention/churn: never disclosed in any source found.
- No reliable year-by-year registered-user series. The 2016 "3M+" comes from a podcast blurb.

## Ownership and corporate events (Next Glass, BeerAdvocate, later)

### Takeaway
Next Glass (Wilmington, NC, founded 2013 by Kurt Taylor) merged with or acquired Untappd in January 2016. It took private-equity growth money from Providence Strategic Growth in 2020, bought BeerAdvocate's assets (Feb 2020) and Hop Culture (Dec 2020), and is now run by CEO Trace Smith. No sale, spin-off or 2024–2026 layoff was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Jan 2016:** Next Glass acquired Untappd, billed as a "strategic merger." Untappd stays its own entity as a subsidiary, and the price was undisclosed — [Porch Drinking, 15 Jan 2016](https://porchdrinking.com/articles/2016/01/15/next-glass-acquires-untapped-in-symbiotic-merger/); [WilmingtonBiz 2016](https://www.wilmingtonbiz.com/technology/2016/01/28/technology/14323)

### Inferences
- The pattern is a consumer app acquired by a B2B beverage-software company, which then monetises the consumer graph through venue SaaS and brand marketing. A PE growth investment (2020) suggests the model is judged a SaaS business, not an ad network.

## Revenue model (Untappd for Business, Insiders, advertising, data)

### Takeaway
Untappd earns money three ways: (1) Untappd for Business, venue SaaS at about $899–$1,199 per venue per year, (2) brand marketing (sponsored badges, home-screen placements, sponsored emails and editorial, recap sponsorships), and (3) Untappd Insiders, a consumer subscription at $5.99/month or $54.99/year. Revenue is undisclosed, and third-party estimates range from $8M to $100M.

### Cited Findings
- **UTFB tiers (third-party prices):** Essentials about $899/yr (or $89.99/mo), capped at 20 non-beer items and 5 user accounts. Premium about $1,199/yr (or $119.99/mo) removes the caps and adds API access and the Wine-Searcher database — [BeerMenus guide, Oct 2025 (competitor)](https://www.beermenus.com/blog/260-guide-to-untappd-for-business); [Untappd Help: Premium vs Essentials](https://help.untappd.com/hc/en-us/articles/13225116244116-Untappd-for-Business-Premium-vs-Untappd-for-Business-Essentials); [Untappd lounge: Premium adds wine/liquor](https://lounge.untappd.com/untappd-for-business-adds-premium-features-for-wine-liquor-and-more)
- **Price increase:** BeerMenus published an article on an Untappd for Business price increase (updated Jan 2024). The specific old and new amounts were not visible — [BeerMenus](https://www.beermenus.com/blog/312-untappd-for-business-price-increase)
- **Official pricing:** the official pricing page gives no prices. It says pricing varies by geography and market, uses a quote form, and lists menu design for website, TV display and print plus POS integrations (Toast, Square, GoTab and more) — [UTFB Get Pricing](https://utfb.untappd.com/get-pricing/)
- **POS integration:** a Toast integration was announced (dated March 2026 per search summary) that auto-updates Untappd menus when Toast changes. It requires Toast's RMS plan. Later updates improved Toast and Square import matching and category mapping — [Next Glass news](https://www.nextglass.co/news/untappd-for-business-announces-a-new-integration-with-the-toast-platform/); [Toast support](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Get-Started-With-the-Untappd-for-Business-Integration); [Untappd Help: Toast/Square imports](https://help.untappd.com/hc/en-us/articles/46140935798548-Better-Toast-Square-Imports-Improved-Matching-Richer-Toast-Descriptions-and-More-Flexible-Square-Category-Mapping)
- **Customer count:** about 15,000 bars and breweries in the UTFB community (single source, Toast's Chinese-language help page) — [Toast support zh-Hans](https://support.toasttab.com/zh-Hans/article/Get-Started-With-the-Untappd-for-Business-Integration)
- **Websites product:** in Feb 2025 UTFB added a website builder for restaurants, bars and taprooms — [Brewbound PR, 25 Feb 2025](https://www.brewbound.com/pr/2025/02/25/introducing-untappd-for-business-websites-enabling-restaurants-bars-and-taprooms-to-easily-build-and-launch-websites)

The original "Supporter" program launched in Dec 2012 — [Untappd blog](https://blog.untappd.com/post/37259088584/become-an-untappd-supporter). - **Advertising:** in-app promotions, home-screen placement (more than 43M monthly impressions), sponsored emails and sponsored editorials. Named advertisers are Diageo, Grolsch and Asahi. Pricing is "flexible" and not published — [Untappd B2C marketing](https://marketing.untappd.com/b2c-marketing-solutions/); [B2B marketing](https://marketing.untappd.com/b2b-marketing-solutions/); [Next Glass sponsorships](https://sponsorships.nextglass.co/). - **Data:** Next Glass's CEO publicly analyses Untappd data on industry trends, and Recappd and Community Awards double as trend PR — [Brewbound Last Call](https://www.brewbound.com/news/last-call-next-glass-ceo-analyzes-untappd-data-to-look-ahead-for-stone-gopuff-lays-off-1500-employees-to-close-76-u-s-warehouses)
- **Revenue estimates (unverified, third-party):** Datanyze $35.1M, LeadIQ $50–100M, Prospeo $8M — [Datanyze](https://www.datanyze.com/companies/next-glass/371619939); [LeadIQ](https://leadiq.com/c/next-glass/5a1d82b324000024005d5403); [Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/c/next-glass)

### Inferences
- Back-of-envelope: about 15,000 venues at roughly $900–$1,200 a year gives about $13–18M in annual UTFB revenue. Ads and Insiders come on top, which is consistent with the $35M Datanyze estimate. This is an inference, not a reported figure.
- For an ice-cream analog the natural paying customers are scoop shops and creameries (menu sync, "Verified" badge, flavour-of-the-day push), plus CPG pint brands (Ben & Jerry's, Jeni's, Salt & Straw) buying sponsored badges and placements. Seasonality matters: summer peaks should drive campaign pricing.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 UTFB price list, and no confirmed details of the Jan 2024 price change.
- No disclosed revenue split between SaaS, ads and Insiders, and no Insiders subscriber count.
- No evidence found of a standalone licensed data product, beyond trend PR and data powering UTFB analytics.

## Criticism, weaknesses and lessons for an ice-cream analog

### Takeaway
The main complaints are rating inflation and hype bias (stouts and hazy IPAs score high, lagers low), "ticker" culture and badge chasing that reward volume and novelty over enjoyment, ratings skewed by intoxication, and venue friction (no free verification, competitor claims of manual menu upkeep). For ice cream the alcohol risk disappears, but hype bias, badge-chasing and moderation load all carry over.

### Cited Findings
- **Style bias:** stouts and imperials skew high because they are trendy, and lagers rarely exceed about 4.25 — [r/NashvilleBeer](https://reddit.fsky.io/r/NashvilleBeer/comments/1j9lifc/meta_uptappd_ratings); see also ["Is 'Not My Style' Guy really ruining Untappd?"](https://drinkingandthinking.substack.com/p/isnot-my-style-guy-really-ruining?open=false)
- **Ticker culture:** critics say Untappd fuels "ticker culture" that favours hype beers and can hurt breweries — [VinePair](https://vinepair.com/articles/untapped-impact-craft-beer/); [RateBeer "Tyranny of the Tickers?"](https://community.ratebeer.com/t/tyranny-of-the-tickers/17429); [Beer Tengoku 2019](https://beertengoku.com/2019/12/01/drunken-ramblings-17-untappd-and-affecting-beer/)
- **Badges and volume:** some badges require multiple check-ins of the same beer, and the author stopped rating partly because ratings degrade on high-volume drinking days — [Porch Drinking, 2017](https://porchdrinking.com/articles/2017/03/01/why-i-stopped-rating-beers-on-untappd/)
- **Social media and consumption:** BeerAdvocate asks whether social media and apps like Untappd changed the way we consume beer — [BeerAdvocate](https://www.beeradvocate.com/articles/11757/drinking-games-have-social-media-and-apps-like-untapped-changed-the-way-we-consume-beer/). No source was found that formally accuses Untappd of encouraging drinking.
- **Comparisons:** VinePair compares crowdsourced beer-rating apps — [VinePair](https://vinepair.com/articles/rating-the-crowdsourced-beer-rating-apps/)
- **Venue cost:** "Is Verified Venue cost worth it?" asks whether the paywall is fair to small venues — [The Brewer Magazine](https://thebrewermagazine.com/tapped-or-untappd-is-verified-venue-cost-worth-it/). ### Inferences
- Lessons for ice cream:
  - Normalise ratings by flavour family, or show "vs. style average," to avoid hype-flavour inflation.
  - Design badges around exploration (new shops, regions, bases) rather than repeat volume, to avoid "eat 5 pints" incentives and health or pester-power criticism, since kids are a likely audience.
  - Keep claiming free and make verification cheap for small scoop shops, which are tighter-margin than bars.
  - Invest early in POS and menu sync because flavours rotate daily.
  - Run a year-end recap as the main growth and sponsorship event.
- The absence of age-gating, since ice cream suits all ages, widens the audience but adds child-privacy (COPPA) obligations that Untappd (17+) avoided — [App Store listing shows 17+](https://apps.apple.com/no/app/untappd/id449141888?l=nb).
