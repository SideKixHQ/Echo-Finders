# Ice Cream Market and Consumer Behavior (for an "Untappd for ice cream" app)

Research method note: about 16 search and fetch calls, done 2026-11-06. Three primary hosts could not be fetched from this session: idfa.org, dairyfoods.com and esmis.nal.usda.gov (USDA NASS) all failed DNS resolution, because they are outside the session's allowed network. ## 1. Market size: US and global, retail/grocery vs foodservice/scoop shop

### Takeaway
US grocery ice cream (Circana, measured retail) is roughly a $16-17B category: about $7.5-8.7B of multi-serve tubs and pints plus about $9.2B of novelties. In dollars it is flat to slightly down, and units are falling. US scoop shops add about $7.1-7.4B (IBISWorld 2025). Global estimates range widely, from about $71B to $121B for 2025, depending on the firm and its scope.

### Cited Findings
- Circana, total ice cream, 52 weeks ended Nov. 2, 2025: $7.5B in dollar sales (down 1% YoY) and 1.55B units (down 3%) — [Dairy Foods via search excerpt](https://dairyfoods.com/articles/96975-ice-cream-sales-stay-hot); see also [Agrimoon summary, Jan 2026](https://agrimoon.com/?p=10220)
- Circana, ice cream/sherbet, 52 weeks ended Nov. 3 (published Jan 2025, so most likely the 2024 period): $8.65B (up 3% YoY) and 1.85B units (up 1%) — [Dairy Foods digital edition, Jan 2025](https://digitaledition.dairyfoods.com/january-2025/market-trends/). **Conflict:** the 2025 figure ($7.5B) is about $1.1B below the 2024 figure, yet is reported as only a 1% decline. The two figures probably use different category definitions, for example with or without sherbet, or a different outlet universe. They are not a like-for-like series.
- Circana, 52 weeks ended March 31, 2024: $7.8B for multi-serve packaged ice cream and $7.7B for novelties — [Dairy Processing, May/June 2024](https://digital.dairyprocessing.com/sosland/dp/dairy-processing-may-june-2024/index.php?startid=16)
- Circana, frozen novelties, 52 weeks ended Dec. 28, 2025: about $9.2B in total novelties dollars with units flat at 1.9B. Within that, frozen novelties were $8.6B (up 3.7%, units up 1% to 1.8B), ice pops $223M (down 13%, units down 16%) and frozen ice cream/ice milk desserts $340M (down 1%) — [Dairy Foods, "Novelty sales produce small gains"](https://www.dairyfoods.com/articles/98029-novelty-sales-produce-small-gains); [Dairy Foods digital edition, Mar 2026](https://digitaledition.dairyfoods.com/march-2026/market-trends/)
- Circana, 12 weeks ended Feb. 23, 2025: dollars flat, ice cream/sherbet units down 1.5%. Superpremium pints "continue to outperform the category as a whole" — [Frozen & Refrigerated Buyer, April 2025, "Rocky road for ice cream"](https://frb.mydigitalpublication.com/articles/rocky-road-for-ice-cream)
- Lighter frozen alternatives were reported up 29% in 2025 while ice cream dipped. This is an aggregator citing Dairy Foods and was not verified — [theworlddata.com](https://theworlddata.com/ice-cream-trends-statistics-in-us/)
- US ice cream stores (IBISWorld NAICS-based "Ice Cream Stores/Shops"): revenue of about $7.4B in 2025 (up 0.9%). An April 2025 edition showed $7.1B, so the figure varies by edition. Employment was about 144,361 in 2025 — [IBISWorld market size](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/market-size/ice-cream-shops/4317/); [IBISWorld employment](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/employment/ice-cream-shops/4317/)
- USDA ERS: US production of ice cream and other frozen dairy fell to 1,386M gallons in 2024, down from a 2003 peak of 1,542M gallons — [USDA ERS Chart of Note](https://ers.usda.gov/data-products/charts-of-note/112892)
- USDA NASS (2025 annual report, from a search excerpt): 2025 regular ice cream production was 845M gallons (down 9.2%) and lowfat 381M gallons (down 21.5%) — [NASS Dairy Products Annual Summary, daryan26.pdf](https://esmis.nal.usda.gov/sites/default/release-files/795874/daryan26.pdf). Regular ice cream in 2023 was 849M gallons, down 8.2% from 2022 — [Texas NASS dairy review 2024](https://data.nass.usda.gov/Statistics_by_State/Texas/Publications/Current_News_Release/2024_Rls/tx-dairy-review-2024.pdf). **Caution:** the 2025 percentage declines are steep, and the 2023 and 2025 regular-gallon figures are nearly identical, which does not sit well with a 9.2% drop from 2024. Verify against the PDF.
- Third-party estimates of the total US market for 2025, which are broader than Circana and include foodservice: Grand View Research $21.64B, Mordor Intelligence $19.51B, MarkNtel $21.11B — [Grand View via GII](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/grvi1941812-us-ice-cream-market-size-share-trends-analysis-by.html); [Statista forecast page](https://statista.com/forecasts/1462356/ice-cream-market-revenue-us)
- Global 2025 estimates: IMARC $80.9B (to $105.6B by 2034); Ken Research $90B (4.6% CAGR to $118B by 2031); Expert Market Research $86.82B; Market Research Future $74.9B; Knowledge Sourcing $70.9B; Grand View Research $121.35B (to $169.4B by 2033) — [IMARC](https://imarcgroup.com/ice-cream-market); [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/global-ice-cream-market); [Expert Market Research](https://www.expertmarketresearch.com/reports/ice-cream-market/companies); [Market Research Future](https://www.marketresearchfuture.com/reports/ai-in-education-market-2691); [Knowledge Sourcing via GII Korea](https://m.giikorea.co.kr/report/ksi1824192-global-ice-cream-market-forecasts-from.html); [Grand View via Dairy Industries](https://www.dairyindustries.com/?p=50757)

### Inferences
- A reasonable framing is roughly $16-17B of US measured-retail frozen dessert (pints and tubs plus novelties) plus roughly $7-7.5B in scoop shops. That is about $24B, which is above the $19-22B third-party US totals, probably because the novelty figures include non-ice-cream frozen treats.
- The category is mature, and price is carrying dollar sales while units fall. The growth pockets are superpremium pints, novelties and "better-for-you" lines. That favors a rating app aimed at the premium/craft end rather than mass tubs.
- Gallon volume has been in long decline since 2003, which is consistent with people eating less but buying more premium product.

### Gaps
- No Euromonitor or Mintel figure was retrievable.
- No clean, like-for-like Circana full-year 2024 vs 2025 series for multi-serve ice cream.
- The NASS 2024 base figures could not be fetched directly.

## 2. Number of US shops; independents vs chains; the craft boom

### Takeaway
IBISWorld counts about 12,781 US ice cream stores (2025, up 2.1% YoY, about 4.1% a year since 2020). Market share concentration is low and Cold Stone is the largest single business. Premium "craft" chains (Jeni's at about 80-90+, Van Leeuwen near 100, Salt & Straw at about 40-50) are small in unit count but are expanding fast and drawing PE/VC money. This resembles craft beer's growth phase.

### Cited Findings
- 12,781 ice cream store businesses in 2025, up 2.1% from 12,523 in 2024. CAGR was about 4.1% over 2020-2025. The definition covers establishments that prepare or serve ice cream, sorbet and gelato — [IBISWorld number of businesses](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/number-of-businesses/ice-cream-stores/4317)
- The industry has "low market share concentration" and its largest business is Cold Stone Creamery — [IBISWorld industry report](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/industry/ice-cream-stores/4317/)
- Jeni's Splendid Ice Creams runs "more than 90 scoop shops" and named an ex-Ben & Jerry's CEO to lead growth — [rallies.ai news item](https://rallies.ai/news/jenis-splendid-ice-creams-names-ex-ben-jerrys-ceo-to-drive-90-store-growth). A crowd-edited chain list gives 80 (2025) — [List of ice cream parlor chains (Wikipedia mirror)](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/List_of_ice_cream_parlor_chains)
- Salt & Straw: "over 40 locations nationwide" with two more planned for fall 2025 — [whatnow.com](https://whatnow.com/?p=309459). The chain list says 50 (2025) — [Wikiwand list](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/List_of_ice_cream_parlor_chains). It was reportedly weighing a sale at a valuation above $200M — [restaurantassociation.com](https://restaurantassociation.com/posts/salt-straw-eyes-200m-deal-to-fund-growth/)
- Van Leeuwen: reported to have opened 29 shops in 2025 with a target of about 100 locations (unverified) — via search summary of [tonetoatl.com, Jan 2025](https://www.tonetoatl.com/2025/01/Van-Leeuwen-Ice-Cream-Opening-Dunwoody-Ponce-City-Market.html); listed in the Restaurant Business Future 50 (2023) — [Restaurant Business](https://restaurantbusinessonline.com/future-50-2023/van-leeuwen-ice-cream)
- Placer.ai treats Dairy Queen, Cold Stone Creamery, Carvel and Ben & Jerry's as the top four ice cream QSR chains — [Placer.ai, Frozen Delights (2024)](https://placer.ai/blog/frozen-delights-exploring-ice-cream-chains-across-america)

### Inferences
- With low concentration and a growing store count, most of the roughly 12.8K establishments are probably independents or small regional operators. That long tail of venues is much like craft breweries, and it is what makes check-ins and venue discovery valuable.
- IBISWorld's count probably excludes many Dairy Queen locations, which are classed as limited-service restaurants, and seasonal stands. The real number of "places you can get a scoop" is likely higher.

### Gaps
- No authoritative independent vs chain split (percentage of units or revenue) was found.
- No reliable current unit counts for Baskin-Robbins, Cold Stone, Dairy Queen, Handel's or Kilwins were retrieved. Company sites or FDD/franchise data would supply them.
- No quantitative source directly compares craft ice cream with craft beer (for instance, Brewers Association-style counts). The parallel is an inference.

## 3. Seasonality and visit frequency

### Takeaway
Visits peak strongly in summer. The peak is more pronounced in colder states such as New York than in Florida. Chain foot traffic hit a 4.5-year high in May 2024, and ice cream and frozen yogurt shops took about 2.1% of all US dining visits in June-July 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Ice cream chain monthly visits reached a 4.5-year high in May 2024. Parlors in colder states (NY) have bigger summer spikes than those in warmer states (FL), where Dairy Queen summer peaks are "much more subdued." Dwell time rose in summer and fell in winter at all brands analyzed — [Placer.ai, Frozen Delights](https://placer.ai/blog/frozen-delights-exploring-ice-cream-chains-across-america)
- Ice cream traffic "depends more on location than the season" — [MediaPost on Placer.ai](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/397541/ice-cream-makes-a-comeback-for-more-than-desser.html)
- Ice cream and frozen yogurt shops took 2.1% of all dining visits in June-July 2026. Froyo chains outperformed ice cream shops in H1 2026 — [Placer.ai on froyo comeback](https://www.placer.ai/anchor/articles/frozen-yogurts-comeback-is-more-than-nostalgia) (via search summary); see also [Placer.ai Big Scoop](https://www.placer.ai/blog/big-scoop-we-all-scream-for-ice-cream) and [Placer.ai top 5 metros for ice cream lovers](https://www.placer.ai/anchor/articles/placer-top-5-metros-for-ice-cream-lovers)

### Inferences
- An app will see strongly seasonal engagement in northern markets. Grocery-pint ratings, which happen at home all year, could smooth winter troughs when stands close.

### Gaps
- No quantified monthly sales index (for example, a percentage of annual sales in June-August) was found.
- No data on how many shops are seasonal or close in winter.
- No per-consumer visit frequency (visits per year) found. IDFA's 2026 survey may contain consumption frequency, but idfa.org could not be fetched.

## 4. Flavor rotation and limited releases

### Takeaway
Premium shops run a high-churn release model. Salt & Straw releases 5 new limited flavors every month, and themed series such as Cereal-sly (March 2026), Vault reissues and the Chef Series (June 2026) rotate out monthly. Ben & Jerry's has retired 200+ flavors and keeps 35+ in its "Flavor Graveyard." This churn closely parallels the release cadence that drives Untappd check-ins.

### Cited Findings
- Salt & Straw "debuts new flavors every month," and its Pints of the Month shipments include 5 new flavors — [Salt & Straw, Pints of the Month](https://saltandstraw.com/products/pints-of-the-month-ice-scream-series); [holiday edition](https://saltandstraw.com/products/pints-of-the-month-holiday)
- March 2026 "Cereal-sly Delicious" series of five cereal flavors — [BroadwayWorld, Feb 2026](https://www.broadwayworld.com/article/SALT-STRAW-with-2-Locations-in-NYC-Offers-Cereal-sly-Delicious-Series-20260228); an April "Vault Series" reissuing five nostalgic flavors — [Tasting Table](https://tastingtable.com/1245780/salt-and-straw-ice-cream-vault-series); a June 2026 Chef Series of five scoops — [Newport Beach Indy](https://www.newportbeachindy.com/?p=69199)
- Ben & Jerry's Flavor Graveyard: physical (Waterbury, VT) and online, opened in 1997 with 4 flavors and now 35 headstones per the October 2025 brand page — [Ben & Jerry's NZ, Oct 2025](https://www.benjerry.co.nz/whats-new/2025/10/flavor-graveyard-depinted); [Tasting Table](https://tastingtable.com/840682/the-truth-about-ben-jerrys-flavor-graveyard). More than 200 flavors have been retired in total — [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/food-drink/visit-to-the-ben-jerrys-flavor-graveyard). Retired flavors sometimes return (Dublin Mudslide) — [Ben & Jerry's US](https://benjerry.com/whats-new/2015/flavor-graveyard-depinted)
- Circana's 2025 New Product Pacesetters: top innovations drove $6.2B in year-one sales across CPG, not ice cream specifically — [AOL/Circana, May 2026](https://lite.aol.com/sports/story/0022/20260521/9724338.htm)

### Inferences
- A shop with a monthly 5-flavor drop alongside a core menu produces about 60 limited flavors a year at a single brand. Multiplied across thousands of independents, the flavor catalog would be large and short-lived. The app therefore needs user-generated flavor entries, not only a curated database.

### Gaps
- No source found for the typical number of flavors in a shop case (often said to be 16-32, unverified).
- No source found on Jeni's collection cadence or Baskin-Robbins "flavor of the month" statistics.
- No count of new grocery ice cream SKUs launched per year (Mintel GNPD or Circana) was found.

## 5. Consumer behavior: popular flavors, adventurousness, demographics, social media, tourism

### Takeaway
IDFA's 2026 survey (Morning Consult, more than 2,200 US adults) has chocolate back at #1, butter pecan above vanilla, and cookies & cream and caramel entering the top five. In 2024 vanilla led, ahead of chocolate and strawberry. Hard data on adventurousness, social-media discovery and ice cream trails was not found in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- IDFA, April 29, 2026: chocolate is again America's favorite flavor, butter pecan has passed vanilla, and cookies & cream and caramel joined the top five. The survey covered more than 2,200 US adults, with Morning Consult — [IDFA release](https://www.idfa.org/news/chocolate-returns-to-the-top-as-americas-favorite-flavor-according-to-idfas-national-ice-cream-trends-survey); [AgWeb](https://www.agweb.com/news/livestock/dairy/chocolate-reclaims-top-spot-americas-favorite-ice-cream-flavor)
- IDFA 2026 toppings and formats: hot fudge 31%, whipped cream 27%, caramel sauce 21%, chocolate sauce 18%. Cones were preferred by 40% — [IDFA release (cones, hot fudge, ice cream trucks)](https://www.idfa.org/news/chocolate-returns-to-no-1-and-butter-pecan-surpasses-vanilla-ahead-of-national-ice-cream-day)
- IDFA May 2024: vanilla #1, chocolate #2, strawberry #3 — [IDFA May 2024 survey](https://idfa.org/resources/ice-cream-frozen-novelty-trends-survey-may-2024); [FoodNavigator](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2024/05/23/america-s-favorite-ice-cream-flavors-and-toppings-ranked/)
- IDFA: 97% of consumers love or like ice cream — [Dairy Foods](https://www.dairyfoods.com/articles/97332-idfa-97-of-consumers-love-or-like-ice-cream)
- An industry leader describes neighborhood ice cream stores as "vital hubs," and the Northeast ranks as the most ice-cream-obsessed region — [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/food-drink/americas-most-ice-cream-obsessed-state-revealed-northeast-crushes-competition)
- Ben & Jerry's and Dairy Queen have over 50% brand popularity among US consumers. Blue Bunny is level with Ben & Jerry's in usage — [Statista brand profiles](https://statista.com/insights/consumer/brand-profiles/2/20/ice-cream/united-states)

### Inferences
- Mainstream tastes are conservative (chocolate, vanilla, butter pecan). The "adventurous" segment that drives craft-flavor ratings is probably a minority, likely urban and younger, matching craft beer's early core. That is an inference, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No quantified data was found on willingness to try novel flavors, on premium buyer demographics, or on TikTok/Instagram as a discovery channel. The full IDFA survey PDF (blocked host) may hold some of this.
- No data was found on ice cream trails (for example state or regional "ice cream trail" programs) or on ice cream tourism.

## 6. Grocery pint landscape: brands, share, barcode data

### Takeaway
Store brands collectively outsell any single brand. Superpremium pints (Ben & Jerry's, Häagen-Dazs, Talenti, Jeni's, Van Leeuwen, Salt & Straw grocery lines) are the outperforming segment. Circana brand-share tables were not retrievable.

### Cited Findings
- Private-label ice cream products together outsell any single brand — [WorldAtlas](https://www.worldatlas.com/industries/the-best-selling-ice-cream-brand-in-the-world-55936.html). Across food and beverage, US private label reached $330B in 2025 — [Circana via pulse.bot](https://www.pulse.bot/food-and-beverage/news/circana-private-label-sales-keep-gaining-share-65298e5d-5b56-4aaa-b486-0aa5de952c3d)
- Superpremium pints outperform the category (Circana, early 2025) — [Frozen & Refrigerated Buyer, Apr 2025](https://frb.mydigitalpublication.com/articles/rocky-road-for-ice-cream)
- Dairy Foods publishes a Circana top-10 ice cream brand table periodically, but the 2025 names and shares were not visible in the snippets — [Dairy Foods digital edition, Jan 2026](https://digitaledition.dairyfoods.com/january-2026/market-trends/)

### Inferences
- Pints carry UPC/EAN barcodes, so scan-to-rate is feasible in grocery. Shop scoops have no barcode, so they need venue plus flavor-name entry. This is an inference about the product, not sourced data.

### Gaps
- No 2025 brand dollar shares were found for Blue Bell, Breyers, Tillamook, Häagen-Dazs, Ben & Jerry's, Talenti or Turkey Hill. Circana tables in Dairy Foods (blocked host) are the likely source.
- No source was retrieved on open barcode data coverage for ice cream, such as Open Food Facts or GS1 US data access. This needs a follow-up search.
