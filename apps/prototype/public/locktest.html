Screen lock test

What keeps working when an iPhone locks. Nothing leaves the phone.

  1. Turn the sound up (ringer switch on) or use headphones.
  2. Tap Start and allow location.
  3. Put the phone down without touching it for one minute. Did the screen stay on by itself?
  4. Press the side button to lock it. Walk for about two minutes, listening.
  5. While it is locked, tap the screen once: if pause/play controls show, pause and play once.
  6. Unlock and come back here. The results appear below. Screenshot them.

Then do it once more from the home screen: Share › Add to Home Screen, open “Lock test” from its icon, and repeat. The two answers can differ.
