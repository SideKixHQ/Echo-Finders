Echo Finders sends you to real places. You are responsible for how you get there and what you
do when you arrive.

- **Do not look at or touch the screen while driving, cycling or operating anything.** On a

## 3. Purchases

### What you can buy
| Plan | Price | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| **Week Pass** | $3.99 | Every story in one city or region, for 7 days from the moment you pay. Does not renew. |
| **City Pass** | $9.99 once | Every story in one city or region, for as long as we offer that city, including stories added later. Does not renew. |
| **All-Access** | $34.99 a year | Every story in every city, on road trips and on flights, for a year. **Renews automatically** (below). |

A City Pass bought while a Week Pass for the same city is still running costs less: the price
you paid for the week counts towards it, and the app shows the reduced price before you pay.

Prices are in US dollars and are shown before you pay. Sales tax is added where it applies.
We may change prices for future purchases; a change never affects something you have already
bought, and a renewal price change is told to you before the renewal (below).

### Who takes your payment
Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe, never to us. Stripe's own
terms apply to the payment. Echo Finders is offered to people in the United States.

### Where your purchase lives
There are no accounts yet. When you buy, your device remembers it, and Stripe keeps the record
of what you paid for. If your device forgets it (a cleared browser, a private window), use
**Settings → Restore purchases** on the device you bought on. Moving a purchase to a different
phone or browser is not possible until accounts exist; when they do, existing purchases will
move with you. If you lose access to a purchase you paid for, contact us (below) with your
receipt and we will sort it out.

### All-Access renews automatically
All-Access is a subscription. **It renews every year at the then-current price, charged to the
card you used, until you cancel.** You can cancel at any time in **Settings → Membership →
Cancel All-Access**, or from the card page Stripe provides (Settings → Payment card). Cancelling
stops the next renewal; you keep All-Access until the end of the year you have already paid
for. We will email you a reminder before each annual renewal, and tell you before a renewal at a
higher price.

### Regions
A Week Pass or City Pass covers one city or region, defined by a distance from its centre (the
Cape Fear Coast pass, for example, covers Wilmington and the beaches within about 42 km). The
app says which pass covers the story you are about to hear. Stories outside every city (on a
parkway, or under a flight path) are part of All-Access.

## 4. The stories

### Accuracy
Every story marked **"True story · verified"** has had its facts checked by a person against the
sources listed with it. Even so, sources disagree and history is revised, and we may get
something wrong. The stories are for interest and enjoyment, not professional, legal, safety or
navigation advice. If you find a mistake, tap **"Not right?"** on the story and tell us; we
correct verified stories when they are shown to be wrong.

Stories marked **"Legend"** are told as legends: we say so out loud, and we do not claim they
are true. Stories marked **"not yet verified"** have not yet been checked.

### True crime
True crime stories are off unless you turn them on. They are told from public records and
published reporting, and anyone accused but not convicted is presumed innocent.

### Sponsored stories
Some stories may be sponsored. A sponsored story says so out loud before it plays, is never
played to children, and never appears in kids mode (`docs/privacy-policy.md`).

### Places change
Buildings are renovated, businesses close, and access changes. A story describes a place as its
sources do; what you find there may differ.
