# Echo Finders — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Echo Finders is a Whatishere.com product.
Questions: [hello@echofinders.com](mailto:hello@echofinders.com)

---

## The short version

Echo Finders needs to know where you are, because the whole app is about telling you what
happened where you are standing. That is the only thing it needs.

- **Your collection stays on your device.** We do not need a copy.
- **We do not record where you stood unless you turn that on.** It is off when you install.
- **You can switch off hands-free walking whenever you like**, and everything else keeps
  working.
- **You can delete everything at any time, from Settings.** Deleted means gone, not hidden.
- **We do not sell your data.** Not to advertisers, not to airlines, not to anyone.

The rest of this document explains each of those properly.

## What we collect, and why

### Your location, while you are using the app
**Why:** to find echoes near you and open them when you arrive. Without it there is no app.
**Where it goes:** it is used on your device and is not sent to us.

### Your location while the app is in the background — only if you turn it on
**Why:** so echoes can open with your phone in your pocket and the screen off, which is the
point of walking mode. You should be looking at the street, not at a phone.
**Default:** off. You are asked the first time you start a hands-free walk, never before.
**If you say no:** echoes still open — you just keep the app on screen while you walk.
**Turning it off later:** Settings → Privacy → Hands-free. Takes effect immediately.
Nothing you have already found is lost.

### Your collection — which echoes you found, and when
**Why:** so you can listen again later, and so the app does not replay something you have
already heard.
**Where it goes:** your device.
**Note:** an echo's own location is public information; it ships inside the app to everyone.
Recording that you found it says very little about you.

### The exact spot you were standing — only if you turn it on
**Why:** it turns your collection into a personal map of where you have been.
**Default:** off.

This is the one piece of data that is about *you* rather than about a place. "I captured
the Federal Hall echo on 14 June" is a fact about an echo. "I was at 40.70691, −74.01128 at
14:32 on 14 June" is a fact about a person, and a year of those is a map of your life. So it
is a separate switch, off until you choose it, and you can delete these positions without
losing your collection.

**Turning it off later:** Settings → Privacy → Remember where I stood. Existing positions
are deleted at the same moment; the collection remains.

### Usage statistics
**On unless you turn it off**, in **Settings → Privacy → Anonymous usage statistics**.
**What is counted:** visits, and a few moments in the app: a story played or its paywall
shown (with the story's category), a checkout started (with the pass and the city) and a
purchase completed (with the pass). Counted by Vercel Web Analytics.
**What is not:** who you are, where you are, or what you have found. No cookies are set,
your IP address is not stored, and the page address is sent without anything after the
"?". The website counts visits the same way.

### Error reports
**Only if you turn them on.** Off by default, in **Settings → Privacy → Send error reports**.
**Why:** when something breaks on your phone, we need to know what broke to fix it.
**What is sent:** the error message, where in the app's code it happened, which version of the
app you have, the page you were on (without anything after the "?") and your browser's type.
**What is not:** where you are, what you have found, or anything that identifies you. At most
five reports per visit. They go to our error-tracking service, Sentry, and are used only to
fix the app.

### Feedback you send us
**Only when you press Send**, in **Settings → Tell us → Send feedback**, or from a story's
card under **Something wrong with this story?**, which also says which story it is about.
**What is sent:** what you wrote, which kind of feedback you chose, the version of the app
and the page you were on. Your email address only if you type it in, so that we can reply.
**What is not:** where you are, what you have found, or anything else about you. It goes to
the same service as error reports, Sentry, and is read by us to fix and improve the app.

### When you buy a pass
**Payments are handled by Stripe.** You type your card details and email address on
Stripe's own checkout page; we never see or store your card number. Stripe's privacy policy
covers what it holds (stripe.com/privacy).
**What we keep:** Stripe tells us which pass you bought, for which city, and when, so the app
can unlock it. Your phone keeps a Stripe customer number so **Restore purchases** can find
your pass again. We do not have an account for you; Stripe's record of the purchase is the
record.
**Refunds and cancelling** are done through Stripe's page, from Settings, or by writing to us.

### Microphone, contacts, photos, health, advertising identifiers
**We do not collect these and do not ask for them.**

## Advertising

**There is no advertising in Echo Finders today**, and no sponsored stories. If we add
sponsored stories later, we will update this policy first, and these rules will hold:

- Every sponsored story says so out loud before it plays.
- Sponsored stories are chosen by **where the route goes**, not by who you are. We do not
  build advertising profiles.
- **No sponsored content reaches anyone under 13**, and none plays in kids mode.
- Advertisers receive counts. They never receive anything identifying you.

---

## What we never do

- Sell or rent your personal data.
- Share your location with advertisers, airlines, tourism boards or data brokers.
- Track you across other apps or websites.
- Keep a continuous trail of your movements. We record arrivals, not journeys — and only
  when you have asked us to.
- Require an account. You can use Echo Finders without telling us who you are.

## Your controls

Everything below is in **Settings → Privacy**, and none of it requires contacting us.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Hands-free | Turns background location on or off. Immediate. |
| Remember where I stood | Turns precise position recording on or off, and deletes existing positions when switched off. |
| Keep my collection | Turn off to stop recording found echoes entirely. |
| Anonymous usage statistics | On by default. Turn off and nothing more is counted, at once. |
| Send error reports | Off by default. Turn on to send us what broke when something breaks. Takes effect immediately. |
| Delete my positions | Removes every standing position, keeps your collection. |
| Delete everything | Removes the lot. |

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Because your data lives on your device, there is no
server copy for us to forget about — but if that ever changes, this policy will say so
plainly before it does.

## Children

Echo Finders is for all ages, and kids mode exists so a child can use it safely.

- Kids mode excludes true crime and any content rated above their age.
- **No sponsored content reaches anyone under 13**, in any mode.
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no account, no
  profile and no advertising identifier to collect. A child should not buy a pass without a
  parent; Stripe's checkout asks for an email address and a card.

Parents and guardians set the age and the categories. Those settings are on the device and
are not sent anywhere.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can delete what is on your phone using the controls above, without
asking us and without waiting. For anything held elsewhere (a purchase at Stripe, a feedback
message you sent) write to [hello@echofinders.com](mailto:hello@echofinders.com) and we will
send you a copy or delete it.

If you are in the UK, EU or EEA: our lawful basis for processing location is **consent**,
which you give by granting the permission and withdraw by revoking it or by switching the
feature off. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything that happened before.

If you are in California: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are
defined by the CCPA/CPRA, so there is nothing to opt out of — but the deletion controls
above are available to you regardless.
